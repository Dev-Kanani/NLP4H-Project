DEPARTMENT OF RADIOLOGY & IMAGING
ULTRASOUND - WHOLE ABDOMEN

INDICATION: Corporate health check-up. Asymptomatic.
TECHNIQUE: Real-time B-mode and colour Doppler sonography performed with a 3.5 MHz curvilinear transducer after overnight fasting. Pelvis scanned transabdominally with full bladder.

LIVER:
Normal in size, measuring 13.9 cm in the mid-clavicular line. Parenchymal echogenicity is mildly increased with preserved visualisation of the portal vein walls and diaphragm, consistent with grade I fatty infiltration. No focal space-occupying lesion. Intrahepatic biliary radicles are not dilated. Portal vein measures 10 mm with normal hepatopetal flow.

GALL BLADDER:
Optimally distended. Multiple mobile echogenic calculi with posterior acoustic shadowing are noted in the lumen, the largest measuring approximately 18 mm. Gall bladder wall shows diffuse thickening, measuring 4 mm. No pericholecystic fluid collection. Sonographic Murphy's sign is negative.

COMMON BILE DUCT:
Measures 4.2 mm at the porta; not dilated.

PANCREAS:
Normal in size and echotexture. Main pancreatic duct is not dilated. No focal lesion or calcification.

SPLEEN:
Measures 9.4 cm. Homogeneous echotexture. No focal lesion.

RIGHT KIDNEY:
Measures 10.3 x 4.5 cm with parenchymal thickness of 1.5 cm. Corticomedullary differentiation is well maintained. No calculus or hydronephrosis.

LEFT KIDNEY:
Measures 10.8 x 4.9 cm, parenchyma 1.6 cm thick. Pelvicalyceal system is not dilated. No renal calculus.

URINARY BLADDER:
Well distended with normal wall thickness. No intraluminal echoes.

UTERUS:
Anteverted, measuring 8.1 x 4.2 x 4.6 cm. Myometrial echotexture is homogeneous. Endometrial thickness 7 mm. No fibroid seen.

OVARIES:
Right ovary 2.8 x 1.9 cm, left ovary 3.0 x 2.1 cm. Both ovaries appear normal.

No free fluid in the abdomen or pelvis.

IMPRESSION:
1. Cholelithiasis - multiple calculi, largest ~18 mm, with diffuse gall bladder wall thickening. No sonographic features of acute cholecystitis.
2. Grade I fatty liver.
3. No other significant abnormality.

ADVICE: Surgical consultation for cholelithiasis is recommended.
