Real-time B-mode sonography of the abdomen and pelvis was carried out as part of the executive health check-up, using a 3.5 MHz curvilinear transducer.

The liver is enlarged, measuring approximately 17.2 cm in its craniocaudal span along the mid-clavicular line, and shows a diffuse increase in parenchymal echogenicity with moderate attenuation of the sound beam, such that the diaphragm is still seen but the walls of the portal venous radicles appear blurred; appearances are in keeping with grade II fatty infiltration. No focal space-occupying lesion is made out within the liver. The intrahepatic biliary radicles are not dilated and the portal vein measures 10 mm at the porta. The gall bladder is adequately distended, its wall is of normal thickness and there is no calculus or sludge within its lumen; the common bile duct is of normal calibre at 4 mm. The pancreas is visualised in its entirety and is normal in size and echopattern, with no peripancreatic collection. The spleen measures 10.4 cm and has a homogeneous echotexture without focal lesion.

The right kidney measures 10.6 x 4.8 cm and the left kidney 10.9 x 5.1 cm; both show normal cortical thickness and well preserved corticomedullary differentiation. A small anechoic simple cortical cyst measuring about 7 mm is noted in the mid pole of the left kidney, with a thin imperceptible wall and posterior acoustic enhancement. There is no evidence of calculus or hydronephrosis on either side. The urinary bladder is well distended with smooth walls and clear contents. The prostate is normal in size, measuring 3.6 x 3.1 x 2.9 cm (volume approx. 17 cc), with homogeneous echotexture. No free fluid is seen in the peritoneal cavity.

In summary, the study shows hepatomegaly with grade II fatty liver and a small simple cortical cyst in the left kidney. Correlation with lipid profile and liver function tests is advised.
