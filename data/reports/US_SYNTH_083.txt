Ultrasound examination of the abdomen and pelvis was performed as part of the annual executive health check using a curvilinear 3.5 MHz transducer. The liver is normal in size, with a span of about 13.5 cm, and shows a uniform echotexture with smooth margins; there is no focal lesion and the intrahepatic biliary radicles are not dilated. The portal vein and hepatic veins appear normal in calibre. The gall bladder is physiologically distended with a thin wall and an echo-free lumen, without any calculus, sludge or polyp, and the common bile duct measures 3 mm. The pancreas is well visualised in its entirety and is normal in size and echotexture, with no peripancreatic collection and a non-dilated main pancreatic duct. The spleen measures 9.8 cm and is homogeneous with no focal lesion. Both kidneys are normal in size and position, the right measuring 10.4 cm and the left 10.8 cm, with well preserved corticomedullary differentiation; neither kidney shows calculus, hydronephrosis or a cortical cyst. The urinary bladder is well distended with a smooth, non-thickened wall and clear contents, and the prostate is normal in size with a volume of approximately 15 cc and homogeneous echotexture. The aorta and IVC are normal in calibre and no para-aortic lymphadenopathy is seen. There is no ascites or pleural effusion. On the whole, no sonographic abnormality is detected in the abdomen and pelvis.
