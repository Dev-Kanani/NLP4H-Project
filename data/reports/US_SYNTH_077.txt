Ultrasound of the whole abdomen was performed for the annual medical check-up. The liver is of normal size and uniform echotexture, with no focal lesion and no intrahepatic biliary dilatation. The gallbladder is well distended and thin walled with no stones within, and the CBD is not dilated. Pancreas and spleen appear normal. In the right kidney, two small echogenic foci with distal acoustic shadowing, the larger measuring 5 mm and the smaller 3 mm, are seen in the lower and mid calyces, consistent with non-obstructing renal calculi. The left kidney shows a single shadowing calculus of 7 mm in the lower pole calyx. Neither kidney shows hydronephrosis, and the ureters are not dilated. Renal size and corticomedullary differentiation are otherwise maintained on both sides. Urinary bladder is well distended with no wall thickening. The prostate is normal in size and echotexture. There is no free fluid.

Impression: Bilateral renal calculi (right 5 mm and 3 mm, left 7 mm) without obstructive uropathy.
