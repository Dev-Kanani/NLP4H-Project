Ultrasound of the whole abdomen was performed as part of annual health check-up.  The liver is of normal size and echotexture with no focal lesion and the intrahepatic biliary radicals are not dilated. Gall bladder is well distended with thin walls and no calculus within it; CBD is of normal calibre. Pancreas and spleen appear normal. The right kidney measures 10.1 x 4.4 cm and is normal in size, position and echogenicity with no calculus or hydronephrosis. The left kidney measures 11.2 x 5.3 cm and shows moderate hydronephrosis with dilated pelvis and calyces, the cause being an echogenic calculus of 11 mm with posterior acoustic shadowing impacted at the pelviureteric junction; the left ureter is not dilated beyond this point. Urinary bladder is adequately distended and normal in wall thickness. Prostate is normal in size and echo pattern. There is no ascites. In summary, left PUJ calculus (11 mm) causing moderate left hydronephrosis, with the rest of the abdomen being normal. Urology opinion is recommended.
