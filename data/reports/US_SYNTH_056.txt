Real-time sonography of the abdomen and pelvis shows the liver to be of normal size and echopattern apart from a well-defined, homogeneously hyperechoic lesion measuring about 14 mm in segment VI of the right lobe, which shows no internal vascularity on colour Doppler and is consistent with a haemangioma; no other focal lesion is identified and the intrahepatic biliary radicles are not dilated. The gall bladder is well distended with a thin wall and no calculi, and the common duct is of normal calibre. Pancreas and spleen appear unremarkable, the spleen measuring 9 cm. Both kidneys are normal in size with preserved corticomedullary differentiation and there is no evidence of calculus, hydronephrosis or cortical cyst. The urinary bladder is distended and smooth in outline. The uterus is bulky, measuring 9.6 x 5.4 x 5.0 cm, and harbours a well-circumscribed hypoechoic intramural fibroid in the posterior wall measuring 2.1 x 1.9 cm which does not distort the endometrium, the latter being 7 mm and regular; both ovaries are normal in size and echotexture for age and there is no free fluid in the pouch of Douglas. Overall impression: hepatic haemangioma in the right lobe and a bulky uterus with a small intramural fibroid, the rest of the study being unremarkable.
