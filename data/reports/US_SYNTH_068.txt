Ultrasound of the abdomen and pelvis was performed as part of the annual health assessment, by the transabdominal route with a full bladder.

The liver is of normal size and echogenicity with smooth margins and shows no focal lesion, the portal and hepatic veins being normal. Gallbladder is well distended, its wall is not thickened and there is no calculus or sludge; CBD is of normal calibre. Pancreas, as far as seen, is unremarkable. Spleen measures 9.1 cm and is normal. Right and left kidneys measure 10.2 cm and 10.5 cm respectively, show normal cortical echoes and have no calculi, with no dilatation of the collecting systems.

The urinary bladder is adequately distended and normal in outline. The uterus is anteverted, measuring 8.6 x 4.9 x 5.1 cm, with a well-defined hypoechoic intramural fibroid of 1.8 x 1.5 cm in the posterior wall; endometrium is central and measures 8 mm. The right ovary is normal. Arising from the left ovary there is a complex cystic lesion measuring 4.1 x 3.3 cm with fine low-level internal echoes and a lacy reticular pattern, without internal vascularity on colour Doppler, possibly a haemorrhagic cyst. A minimal amount of free fluid is noted in the pouch of Douglas, likely physiological.

Impression: Left ovarian complex cyst (4.1 cm), possibly haemorrhagic - suggest follow-up scan in the post-menstrual phase (day 5-7). Small intramural uterine fibroid. Minimal physiological POD fluid.
